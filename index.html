    Emberfront

    A fast territory war where every kingdom builds itself.

    • Click unclaimed or enemy land to attack along your whole shared border.
    • The slider sets how much of your army each attack takes.
    • Troops regrow fastest around half full. Sitting at max wastes growth.
    • Villages, farms, mines and towers appear on their own. Houses raise your troop cap, farms speed up growth, towers make nearby land costly to take.
    • Captured towns burn. Take an enemy capital and a quarter of their army scatters.
    • Every 10 seconds a human walks out of your capital. The soldier slider sets how many of your people serve as soldiers: villagers walk into the capital to train, and newcomers fill the ranks first. Soldiers don't fight yet.
    • Villagers gather wood, stone and copper and carry it to your capital. Forests are rich in wood, mountains in stone, deserts in copper.

    Drag to pan · scroll or pinch to zoom · keys 1–9 set the attack % · Space centers on your capital · P pauses
