    Emberfront

    A fast territory war where every kingdom builds itself.

    • Click unclaimed land to push your border: settling costs 0.36 gold a tile, and your land pays a small tax. A small menu asks how much of your gold the push spends: 25% to 100%.
    • Your income is tax: 0.1 gold a second from every housed villager (soldiers pay none), plus a little for every tile of land. Every 20 seconds a human walks out of your capital.
    • Everyone needs a home: while you are short of houses, a newcomer first builds himself a hovel near the rest of town. Houses nobody needs for a while stand empty.
    • Nothing lies on the map to gather: wood, stone and copper come from plantations, quarries and copper mines (your first of each is free). Research lets them join into bigger ones. Your villagers build homes and rest in them.
    • Open a building menu on the right (urban development, military, resources, society) to place farms, barracks, watchtowers and more. The same buildings side by side join up.
    • The soldier slider raises militia at the castle: unpaid at home, they rest in your houses and come out when called, and can go to war with you. A barracks trains them into soldiers, who are paid wages. Click a watchtower to call 5 militia to guard it.
    • Click another kingdom for diplomacy. Allies open their land to your soldiers. Go to war with as many soldiers as you choose: an attack masses your armies on their border in a crisis; after 30 seconds (time for both sides to react, or to call it off) Begin offensive makes it war, fought along the whole front. Break the castle and the whole kingdom is yours. A naval base lets you settle islands by ship (⚓ Naval settlement in the settle menu) and land armies from the sea (⚓ Naval invasion): ships, 5 men each, carry them to the coast you point at.
    • Only at war do soldiers fight, and watchtowers and your castle shoot at enemies on your land. Land an attack takes is only occupied (hatched) until peace: drive the enemy out and take it back, then counter-attack. Every army has its own orders in the Armies list (top right, in peace and in war): press 📍 Assign border, then point at the border on the map (it lights up: a part of it, or a capital to besiege it; shift-click for the whole stretch) and click: the army travels there from wherever it is, in peace (not a crisis) or at war, by boat between your naval bases where there is no way by land, and you can reassign it any time. Toggle Attack or Defend: a defending army falls back to the closest ground of yours that no enemy tower reaches and holds a line there. New soldiers join the army that lost the most; an army across the sea is carried over for free in the boats of your naval bases (5 men each); builders and smiths cross between bases too. Invaders go for the watchtowers that cover them first. Invading is slow.
    • Each war has a button at the top: offer peace; you answer an army massing on your border by assigning your armies to it. The war bar clears the moment the war is over. Villagers caught near a fight either run or take up arms.
