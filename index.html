    Emberfront

    A fast territory war where every kingdom builds itself.

    • Click unclaimed or enemy land to push your border. Settling costs 1 gold a tile; enemy land costs more the stronger they are. The top slider sets how much of your gold each push spends.
    • You earn 5 gold a second, plus 0.1 in tax from every villager (soldiers pay none). Every 10 seconds a human walks out of your capital.
    • Everyone needs a home: while you are short of houses, a newcomer first builds himself a hovel near the rest of town. Houses nobody needs for a while stand empty.
    • Villagers gather wood, stone and copper. Forests are rich in wood, mountains in stone, deserts in copper. Nothing grows back.
    • Open Buildings (the hammer) to place farms, barracks, watchtowers and coin minters. The same buildings side by side join up.
    • A barracks lets you raise soldiers with the second slider. Click a watchtower to call 5 soldiers to guard it; towers shoot enemy soldiers nearby.
    • Strength counts your land, people and gold. Captured towns burn; take an enemy capital and you loot a quarter of its gold.

    Drag to pan · scroll or pinch to zoom · keys 1–9 set the attack % · B opens buildings · Esc stops placing · Space centers on your capital · P pauses
