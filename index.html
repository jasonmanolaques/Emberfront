    Emberfront

    A fast territory war where every kingdom builds itself.

    • Click unclaimed land to push your border: settling costs 1 gold a tile. A small menu asks how much of your gold the push spends: 25% to 100%.
    • You earn 5 gold a second, plus 0.1 in tax from every villager (soldiers pay none). Every 10 seconds a human walks out of your capital.
    • Everyone needs a home: while you are short of houses, a newcomer first builds himself a hovel near the rest of town. Houses nobody needs for a while stand empty.
    • Villagers gather wood, stone and copper. Forests are rich in wood, mountains in stone, deserts in copper. Nothing grows back.
    • Open Buildings (the hammer) to place farms, barracks, watchtowers and coin minters. The same buildings side by side join up.
    • A barracks lets you raise soldiers with the soldier slider. Soldiers are paid wages. Beyond the barracks' room the slider raises militia: unpaid until called up, they patrol and guard watchtowers. Click a watchtower to call 5 militia to guard it.
    • Click another kingdom for diplomacy. Allies open their land to your soldiers. Go to war with as many soldiers as you choose: a raid burns their buildings for loot; an attack masses your armies on their border in a crisis, and your word in the war bar makes it war, fought along the whole front. Break the castle and the whole kingdom is yours.
    • Only at war do soldiers fight, and watchtowers and your castle shoot at enemies on your land. Land an attack takes is only occupied (hatched) until peace: drive the enemy out and take it back, then counter-attack.
    • Each war has a button at the top: recall a raid, offer peace, or, when an army marches on you, intercept it. Villagers caught near a fight either run or take up arms.
